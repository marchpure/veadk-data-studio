# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We want Byaan to be a practical, respectful project for people building data tools, database integrations, MCP workflows, and local-first software.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when impact differs from intent.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce or understand your point.
- Avoid posting secrets, private schemas, credentials, or sensitive customer data.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Doxing or sharing private information.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing exploit details for unresolved vulnerabilities in public channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or ban contributors who violate this code of conduct.

Report conduct concerns to security@byaan.ai.
